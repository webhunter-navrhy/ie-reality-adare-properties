A well-located 3-bedroom home with attached garage and generous rear garden

John Giltinane & Associates are delighted to bring to the market Seanachluain, 8 Desmond Drive, Adare, Co. Limerick.

Situated in a great location within the popular village of Adare, this property offers well-proportioned accommodation together with an attached garage and a generously sized rear garden.

Outside

The property benefits from an attached garage, providing useful additional storage or potential for a variety of uses, subject to any necessary permissions.

To the rear is a generously sized back garden, offering excellent outdoor space and potential for gardening, entertaining or family use.

Location

Desmond Drive is situated in the highly sought-after village of Adare, renowned for its picturesque setting, thatched cottages, village amenities and excellent local facilities. The property is conveniently located for access to shops, schools, restaurants, cafés and other amenities, while Limerick City is also readily accessible.

This property presents an excellent opportunity to acquire a well-located home in one of County Limerick's most desirable villages.
